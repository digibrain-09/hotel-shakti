Residential Projects

Residential projects are an important part of our business, whether you need a new or replacement window, a whole house or an extension. We have a wide selection of products available that are both aesthetically appealing and will also save you money on your energy bills. We can provide you with just the windows, or arrange a complete supply and installation service for you.

Commercial Projects

First Glass Windows undertake commercial projects and we can handle any project from changing only a window or a door to large multi storey buildings.
We supply and install the following
•Commercial Aluminium
•Windows and doors
•Shop fronts
•Curtain Walling

Repair Service

First Glass Windows offer a fast and reliable repair service for faulty windows and doors.
We supply and fit all types of
•Hinges
•Handle (all key locking)
•Mechanism
•Key locking barrels
•Window sealed units
Above are only a few of the services and repairs First Glass Windows can carry out.
